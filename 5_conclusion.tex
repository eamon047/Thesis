\section{Conclusion}

In this thesis, we proposed
Conditional Geometric Resampling (CGR), which is a technique to give unbiased or small-bias estimators for $w_{t,i}^{-1}$.
%Conditional Geometric Resampling (CGR), which includes three variants (CGR~I, II-unbiased, II-biased), a technique employed in FTPL to give unbiased or small-bias estimators for $w_{t,i}^{-1}$.
The most important contribution is that, CGR is the first algorithm to enable FTPL to achieve BOBW with
average
%amortized
complexity of $O\qty(K\log K)$ without sacrificing the regret.
For the second version of CGR with perturbations following Fr\'{e}chet distribution with shape $\alpha = 2$, we provided a bound of $O\qty(K)$ for the expected number of resampling given the chosen arm, and proved the BOBW property of CGR~II-biased with the maximum number of resampling $\left\lceil(K\lor 4)\log t\right\rceil$.
We empirically demonstrated that CGR not only significantly improves runtime efficiency, but also improves the regret from FTPL with the original geometric resampling. 

One of our important furture directions is to extend the BOBW guarantee and the improved complexity of FTPL with CGR to more general setting, such as $m$-set semi-bandit problems, where $m\in[K]$ arms are chosen at each round. 
At the first step, we expect to prove that FTPL with original GR with Fr\'{e}chet distribution can achieve $O(\sqrt{mKT})$ optimal adversarial regret \citep{audibert2014regret} and a logarithmic stochastic regret in $m$-set semi-bandits, demonstraing the BOBW optimality in this setting. 
In addition, we can consider extending such results to other Fr\'{e}chet-type perturbations, e.g., Pareto distribution with specific shape parameter, since it was also shown that FTPL with this distribution can also achieve the BOBW optimality in MAB \citep{pmlr-v247-lee24a}. 
Then, though the current CGR is tailored to the MAB, we expect that it can be extended to $m$-set semi-bandits, with which FTPL can achieve a favorable complexity without sacrificing the regret guarantees.
